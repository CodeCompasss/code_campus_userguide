# Puzzle

### What is it?

Puzzle is a collection of logical and mathematical puzzles designed to improve problem-solving skills. It is a great resource for preparing for interviews that include brainteasers and logic-based questions.

### Why Use Puzzle?

- **Improve Logical Thinking**: Enhance your ability to solve complex problems.
- **Fun and Challenging**: Solve puzzles that are both entertaining and educational.
- **Interview Preparation**: Practice puzzles commonly asked in interviews.

### Getting Started

1. Visit the [Puzzle website](https://puzzle.com/).
2. Choose a category and start solving puzzles.
3. Track your progress and improve over time.

### Tools and Resources

- **Puzzle Books**: Refer to books like "The Art of Problem Solving."
- **Online Forums**: Join communities to discuss solutions.
- **Practice Platforms**: Use websites like Brilliant.org for additional puzzles.
