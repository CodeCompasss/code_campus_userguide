# Pramp

### What is it?

Pramp is a platform that offers free mock interviews for software engineers. It pairs you with other developers to practice coding, system design, and behavioral interviews.

### Why Use Pramp?

- **Free Mock Interviews**: Practice with peers in a realistic interview setting.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Covers coding, system design, and behavioral interviews.
- **Feedback**: Get constructive feedback to improve your performance.

### Getting Started

1. Visit the [Pramp website](https://www.pramp.com/).
2. Sign up for a free account.
3. Schedule a mock interview based on your preferences.
4. Practice and improve with feedback from your peers.

### Tools and Resources

- **Coding Platforms**: Practice on LeetCode, CodeChef, or HackerRank.
- **Interview Guides**: Use resources like Tech Interview Handbook for preparation.
- **Community**: Join forums to connect with other learners.
