# IndiaBX

### What is it?

IndiaBX is a platform designed to help Indian students and professionals prepare for technical interviews. It offers resources tailored to the Indian job market, including coding problems, mock interviews, and resume tips.

### Why Use IndiaBX?

- **Localized Content**: Focused on the Indian job market.
- **Comprehensive Resources**: Covers coding, system design, and behavioral interviews.
- **Mock Interviews**: Practice with industry professionals.

### Getting Started

1. Visit the [IndiaBX website](https://indiabx.com/).
2. Sign up for a free account.
3. Explore the coding problems and interview guides.
4. Schedule mock interviews to get feedback.

### Tools and Resources

- **Coding Platforms**: Practice on LeetCode, CodeChef, or HackerRank.
- **Resume Tips**: Learn how to craft a job-winning resume.
- **Community**: Join forums to connect with other job seekers.
