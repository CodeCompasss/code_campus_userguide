# Take U Forward

### What is it?

Take U Forward is a platform that provides resources for technical interview preparation. It includes video tutorials, coding problems, and study plans tailored for software engineering roles.

### Why Use Take U Forward?

- **Structured Learning**: Follow a step-by-step roadmap for interview preparation.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Covers data structures, algorithms, and system design.
- **Community Support**: Join a community of learners and mentors.

### Getting Started

1. Visit the [Take U Forward website](https://takeuforward.org/).
2. Explore the video tutorials and coding problems.
3. Follow the recommended study plans.

### Tools and Resources

- **Coding Platforms**: Practice on LeetCode, CodeChef, or HackerRank.
- **Mock Interviews**: Use platforms like Pramp or Interviewing.io for practice.
- **Community**: Join forums to connect with other learners.
